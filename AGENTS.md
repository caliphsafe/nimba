# Nimba project rules

- Never add emojis to the storefront, admin, buttons, messages, or other interface content unless the user explicitly requests them. Use SVG artwork or icons.
- Preserve the Nimba palette and Calistoga headings unless the user requests a change.
- Original carved icons are contemporary brand illustrations inspired by the Nimba profile and carved details. Do not present them as historical Baga symbols.
- Keep public copy in English and French and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Featured slots 1–4 must drive the same ordering on Home and at the top of Shop.
- Keep garment close-up photography and the editorial lookbook deferred until the user supplies suitable images. Do not invent product details or substitute mock photography for actual Shopify products.
- Keep navigation text-only. Use the custom carved icons within page content. The Home hero must prioritize large live Shopify product imagery.
